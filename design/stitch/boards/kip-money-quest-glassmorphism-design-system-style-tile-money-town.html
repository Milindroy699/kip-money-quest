04. Surface Physics

Glass (75%) vs. Near-Solid Spec

WCAG AA 4.5:1 Strict

Rule: Frosted glass panels are calibrated to exactly 75% opacity with 1px white inner borders; text containers use Near-solid (94%+) with dark ink typography for flawless glare contrast.

Glass Panel (75%) HUD / Nav / Modals

Layer 3: Translucent HUD

  • • Fill: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75) (Strict 75%)
  • • Inner Border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.50)
  • • Backdrop Filter: blur(28px) saturate(180%)
  • • Shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(30, 42, 58, 0.12)
  • • Corner Radius: 24px continuous curve
✨ Floats cleanly above L2 blurred coins without muddying legibility.
Near-Solid Card (94%) Text & Charts

Layer 4: Crisp Readability

  • • Fill: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)
  • • Backdrop Filter: blur(12px)
  • • Border: 1px solid rgba(230, 235, 243, 0.90)
  • • Contrast Ratio: 11.4:1 on dark ink body text (WCAG AAA)
  • • Body Text Fill: Ink Navy (#1E2A3A) strictly ≥ 4.5:1
🛡️ Guarantees adolescent readers zero eye strain under harsh outdoor light.
