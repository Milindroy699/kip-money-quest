# Competitor and Inspiration Review

| Product | Category | Borrow | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duolingo** | Learning | Path map, streaks, leagues, mascot-voiced notifications, Rive-animated characters, value before signup, streak freeze | Hearts that block learning; guilt-trip nudges |
| **Finch** | Self-care pet | A companion that grows as you grow; gentle, no-shame tone | Low challenge |
| **Zogo** | Finance learning | Bite-size finance modules with rewards | Thin gameplay, quiz-only |
| **Greenlight (Level Up) / Step** | Teen banking | Teen-specific framing, parent involvement | Bank-account-first product |
| **Zerodha Varsity** | Indian investing education | Depth and Indian-market accuracy for Worlds 6–8 | Text-heavy format |
| **SIFMA Stock Market Game / Investopedia Simulator** | Simulated trading | School competitions, simulated portfolios | Ranking by raw returns, which teaches gambling |
| **Subway Surfers** | Runner | Chaser, 3 lanes, rails, short replayable runs, juicy feedback | — |
| **Clash Royale / Marvel Snap** | Card battles | 2–3 minute async matches, ladders, clear card stats | Pay-to-win |
| **Balatro** | Roguelike cards | The multiplier reveal that makes multiplication thrilling (our compounding reveal) | — |
| **Pokémon TCG / FIFA Ultimate Team** | Collectibles | Readable stat bars, collection completion pull | Loot-box randomness |
| **Monopoly Go / Coin Master** | Social casual | Live social events, village/city meta-builder (our Future City) | Gambling-style spins |
| **Cashflow (board game)** | Finance board game | Assets vs liabilities thinking | — |
| **Brilliant** | Interactive learning | Manipulable explainers instead of text | — |

## Our differentiators
1. **The retention system teaches the principle.** The Knowledge SIP streak, the compounding multiplier, the emergency-fund freeze and Dividend Day each model the concept they reward.
2. **One honest simulation** behind runner, cards and duels. Scores reward goal-fit, risk-aware outcomes.
3. **Principle → Product → Philosophy** in every lesson, India-first.
4. **Future You** as the north star. The player's own goal, visualised and sharpening as they learn.
