# Character Bible

## Kip the Tortoise — mascot and teacher

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Look | Young green tortoise, oversized round **specs**, small backpack, golden-honey shell with hexagon scutes |
| Signature | **Shell growth rings.** Kip's shell gains a glowing ring for every full streak week. Compounding, visualised on the mascot |
| Personality | Calm, warm, witty, a little nerdy. Loves "fun facts". Never shames. Celebrates effort over outcomes |
| Voice | Short sentences. Gentle humour. Uses "we". Explains with everyday Indian examples (chai, cricket, auto fares, pocket money) |
| Catchphrases | "Slow and steady compounds." · "Let's look before we leap." · "Future You says thanks!" |
| Why a tortoise | Aesop's tortoise beat the hare through consistency. Tortoises live 100+ years, a natural long horizon |

**Expressions (Phase 1, Skia):** `happy`, `talk`, `think`, `worry`, `cheer`.
**Rive state machine (Phase 3):** idle, talk (lip flap), think, cheer, worry, facepalm, celebrate, sleepy (for nudges), and eye tracking toward the touch point.

**Kip does not:**
- recommend specific real-world stocks or funds
- promise returns
- mock the player

## Dash the Hare — the lovable foil

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Look | Orange hare, sunglasses pushed up on the head, sneakers, phone always in hand |
| Personality | Fast, funny, impatient. Chases hot tips, trades intraday, pulls money in and out |
| Role | Makes the mistakes so the player doesn't have to. Many lesson hooks start with "Dash just…" |
| Arc | Over the season Dash learns. By World 6, Dash becomes the hedging and arbitrage expert, proving anyone can change |

## The Melt — inflation

- A drifting purple fog with a wobbly smile. It shrinks any coin it touches.
- It is the chaser in Kip Run, like the inspector in Subway Surfers.
- It is not evil, just relentless, like real inflation. **You can't stop it. You can outgrow it.**

## Visual style
- **Style:** flat 2D, thick rounded outlines, warm cream backgrounds, saturated accents.
- **Palette:** Kip green `#2DBE7E`, shell honey `#F2B544`, ink `#1E2A3A`, cream `#FFF8EC`, Melt purple `#8B5CF6`, Dash orange `#FF8A3D`.
- **Tone:** friendly and premium, never babyish. Teens 16–19 must not feel talked down to.
