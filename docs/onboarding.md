# Onboarding and Activation

**Goal:** the player feels the core promise ("Future You gets richer and wiser") within 60 seconds, plays the core game within 2 minutes, and commits to a daily habit before signup. This follows the Duolingo pattern of value before signup.

## Flow (built in Phase 1: steps 1–6)

| # | Screen | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hi, I'm Kip** | Kip waves; asks the player's name and age band (13–15 / 16–19) | Personal from second one; sets Explorer or Builder track |
| 2 | **What's your dream?** | Pick a goal (laptop, bike, travel, college, own business, free at 40) and a target age. A foggy Future You appears | Anchors everything to the player's own win |
| 3 | **Kip vs Dash** (magic moment) | Dash keeps ₹500/month in cash. Kip runs a ₹500/month SIP. The player drags a time slider (5–40 years) and watches Kip's curve bend upward while The Melt shrinks Dash's pile. A "hypothetical 12% p.a." label stays visible | The "aha": compounding + inflation in one gesture |
| 4 | **First run** | 45-second Inflation Chase tutorial with inline tips | Core fun early |
| 5 | **First cards** | Cash and Fixed Deposit cards flip in. FinIQ animates 300 → 320 | First collection and progress hit |
| 6 | **Commit** | Pick a daily goal (3/5/10 min) and a reminder time, then tap "Start my Knowledge SIP". The reminder is scheduled | Habit and commitment device |
| 7 | **Save progress** (built, cloud pending; see [accounts.md](accounts.md)) | Offered after the activation milestone (3 lessons and a run), not during onboarding. Sign in with Apple or Google. Under-18s hand the phone to a parent, who consents and signs in with their own account. The parent weekly digest comes later | Compliance and parent buy-in |

## Activation
- **Definition:** 3 lessons + 1 run + reminder set within 72 hours of install, plus a return on Day 1.
- **First-week guided path:** Day 1 Money Town, Day 2 first Melt lesson, Day 3 first Dividend Day preview, Day 7 first shell ring.

## Principles
- No signup wall before the magic moment.
- Every screen has one primary action.
- The "hypothetical, educational" label is shown on every projection, from the first chart.
- Skipping is allowed but nudged: "Kip will keep your seat warm."
