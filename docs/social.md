# Competition and Social Play

All social features are **safe by design** for minors:
- No free-text chat. Players use only preset reactions and emotes.
- Friends are added by invite code or contacts, with parental controls.
- Usernames are moderated.

| Feature | Phase | How it works | Teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly leagues** | 1 (local preview) → 2 | Cohorts of 30, ranked by weekly XP. Tiers: Piggy Bank → Fixed Deposit → Bond → Index → Blue Chip → Compounder. Top 7 promote, bottom 5 demote | Consistency (XP includes the streak multiplier) |
| **Market Duels** | 2 | Async PvP. Same goal brief and seed. Build a 5-card portfolio and reveal 5 Market Years. Scored on goal + drawdown + liquidity. Elo ladder | Goal-fit, risk-aware allocation |
| **Friend streaks** | 2 | A shared Knowledge SIP with a friend. Both must be active daily | Accountability |
| **Investment Clubs** | 3 | Squads of 5 pool XP into a Club Fund that compounds weekly, and complete club quests | Pooling (how mutual funds work) |
| **Live events** | 3 | E.g. *Market Mayhem Weekend*: a simulated crash where players who hold and rebalance calmly win | Behaviour under stress |
| **Classroom mode** | 3 | Teacher dashboard, class leagues, inter-school **Investing Olympiad** | Distribution through schools |
| **Shareables** | 2 | Future You card, world certificates, FinIQ milestones, formatted for Instagram and WhatsApp stories | Pride, organic growth |

## Scoring rule for every competitive mode
Rank by **goal-based, risk-adjusted** outcome, never raw return. A reckless all-in portfolio that happens to win once must lose on average. Speculation is taught, never rewarded.
